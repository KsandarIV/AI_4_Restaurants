Booking a table by talking: the idea in plain words

Picture a very attentive waiter who never sleeps, never mishears a time and never forgets a booking. Our voice assistant does exactly that, with four helpers:

👂 The earsThey notice when you start talking and when you pause. No buttons: just talk.
✍️ The translatorIt turns your voice into written text, instantly, on our own computer (nothing goes to the internet).
🧠 The brainIt reads the request, checks free tables, the menu and opening hours, and decides the answer — like the best receptionist would.
🗣️ The voiceIt reads the answer aloud, starting to speak before it has even finished thinking.

An example call

Welcome! To manage bookings I need your name and a phone number.

Mario Rossi, 333 123 4567.

I understood: Mario Rossi, phone 3 3 3, 1 2 3, 4 5 6 7. Is that correct?

Yes.

Thank you Mario! How can I help?

A table for two tomorrow at eight… no wait, half past eight!

Table for two tomorrow at 20:30. Do you confirm? … Booking confirmed, code R E S, 9 A 8 B 2 C.

Note: when you said “no wait”, the assistant stopped and listened again — like a polite person would. And your name and phone number never reach the AI model: they are stored by the restaurant's system only so the staff can contact you.

Why it suits the restaurant

  • It always answers, even at midnight with the phone ringing.
  • Nothing to install for customers: just the website.
  • Everything in-house: no subscription to external services, no data leaves the premises.
